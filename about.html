Our Origin Story

Built from
Lived Frustration

In 2021, co-founders Meera Krishnamurthy and Rahul Desai were both working from home — Meera as a product designer, Rahul as a strategy consultant.

Their shared café was too noisy. Their nearest coworking space looked like a grey airport lounge. The coffee was dreadful. So they signed a lease, hired a Bengaluru architect they admired, and spent six months obsessing over acoustics, light quality, material texture, and a single perfect espresso recipe. DESKORA opened in March 2021 with 40 desks and a waiting list of 200.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

01

Intentional Design

Every surface, seat, and corner was chosen with deliberate care — not for aesthetics alone, but to serve the act of thinking deeply.

02

Radical Hospitality

We greet every member by name. Our staff are trained not as customer service agents, but as hosts — warm, attentive, and genuinely curious.

03

Curated Community

The best coworking spaces are not just rooms with desks — they are ecosystems of collaboration, peer mentorship, and serendipitous opportunity.

04

Coffee as Craft

Our head barista sources directly from three estates in Karnataka and Coorg. Coffee is not an afterthought here — it is a cornerstone of the experience.

05

Sustainability First

Rainwater harvesting, solar panels, zero single-use plastic, and a full composting programme. We believe beautiful spaces should also be responsible ones.

06

Accessible Excellence

Premium should not mean exclusionary. Our student passes and flexible day rates ensure that great workspace is accessible to everyone building something meaningful.
